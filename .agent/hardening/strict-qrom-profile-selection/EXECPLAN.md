Determine whether the exact repository CMS policy envelope, a one-level Ligerito source expression, and E384/E512 challenges can produce a defensible strict post-quantum profile for the M4 transaction relation. Retain exact arithmetic and theorem-premise evidence even if the answer is no.

## Surprises and discoveries

- The smallest one-term local screens are q=130 for both fields, but their margins are only about 0.51 and 0.60 bits. Two equal source terms already require q=131.
- E512 fits the live power-of-two extension-degree trait shape while E384 does not, but E512 is not implemented and its conditional proof grammar is larger.
- Direct BCS statistical ZK at lambda=512 cannot reach strict 128 bits even before adding the underlying HVZK error.
- HVZK-WHIR supplies the right soundness/ZK direction at theorem-shape level, but its RBR-knowledge notion is a strict relaxation of CMS's knowledge premise.
- CMS's `t` is an augmented game-query budget; the exact conversion from an external adversary budget is hidden in `O(q log ell)` and cannot be silently set to zero.

## Decision log

- Use exact `Fraction` comparisons for every gate; decimal bits are display only.
- Treat `12*t^2*epsilon + 48*t^3/2^512 + 2*K^2/2^512` as an exact local policy corollary, not an exact CMS theorem statement.
- Set `K=M` only inside conditional source screens. Do not claim it bounds the exact CMS base-game arity until the transcript is compiled and counted.
- Keep all non-source ledger terms explicit and null instead of allocating arbitrary equal-error placeholders.
- Keep the 512-MiB oracle limit as a search resource bound, not an architecture or production proof-size limit.
- Keep HVZK-WHIR selected false and CMS knowledge applicability null.
- Do not pin live-owner sources until the root requests a quiescent repin.

## Outcome

The package establishes a quantified no-go for selecting a current profile: the arithmetic source screens pass conditionally, but no exact M4 IOP/RBR premise, complete-ZK bound, CMS compiler/query accounting, concrete hash bridge, or full union ledger exists. `production_profile`, `selected_query_count`, `overall_total`, and `strict_pq_bits` remain null; all capabilities remain false.
