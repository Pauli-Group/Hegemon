# Union-sensitive regular fibers close the compressed-stress dimension<=21 branch

Status: independently reviewed mathematical derivation with exact arithmetic.
The original frozen evidence remains unchanged. This proves
a conditional branch of the unrestricted weighted-budget inequality, not a
universal stress bound, accepted-byte extraction, or production security.

The arbitrary fixed sources, actual Goldilocks coset, degree387 responses,
five base-field label coordinates, and actual full-support weights remain
unchanged. The new ingredient counts a regular matrix fiber according to
the actual union of its chosen bad813-subsets.

## 1. Refined minimum-weight count for a selected spanning family

Let a projectively distinct selected family of weight-a vectors span an
r-dimensional linear code of minimum distance at least a. Let n be the
size of the union of their supports, so the code has no unused coordinates.
As before, write A_a(d)=choose(a+d-1,d-1) for d>=1 and A_a(0)=0.
For every integer ell>=0 with n>ell*a, the family size m obeys

    m <= n/(n-ell*a) * A_a(r-ell).                        (1)

Here r>ell follows automatically: a basis from the selected weight-a
vectors covers the union, so n<=r*a. The ell=0 case is the already proved
minimum-weight multiplicity bound.

Induct on ell. For a coordinate x, let the subfamily zero at x span a code
of dimension r_x=r-delta_x, with union size n_x. Since x is an active
coordinate, delta_x>=1. Extend a basis of that subfamily to a basis of the
whole code using delta_x selected weight-a vectors. All coordinates missing
from its union are covered by those additional vectors, so

    n_x >= n-delta_x*a.                                  (2)

If delta_x>=ell, its size m_x is at most A_a(r-ell). If delta_x<ell,
equation (2) gives n_x>(ell-delta_x)*a, and induction on the zero subfamily
with index ell-delta_x gives

    m_x <= n_x/[n_x-(ell-delta_x)*a] * A_a(r-ell)
        <= (n-delta_x*a)/(n-ell*a) * A_a(r-ell)
        <= (n-a)/(n-ell*a) * A_a(r-ell).

The middle inequality uses that y/(y-c) decreases for y>c>0.
The same final bound covers delta_x>=ell, including an empty zero subfamily.
Each selected word has exactly n-a zero coordinates, so

    m(n-a)=sum_x m_x <= n(n-a)/(n-ell*a) * A_a(r-ell).

For ell>=1, n>a, allowing cancellation and proving (1). This proof concerns
the selected family itself; it does not assume that every minimum-weight
word of the spanned code was selected.

## 2. A uniform small-union MCA count on actual points

The primary source is [BCHKS, Theorem4.6, pp.28-29](https://www.math.toronto.edu/swastik/rs-proximity-gaps-2025.pdf),
checked directly in this pass. Apply its support-wise bound over K=F_(p^5)
with curve degree M=1, response degree d=387, n=1707, and agreement813.
Its hypotheses hold because gamma=1-813/1707 is positive and
813^2>387*1707. Its multiplicity is at most4096: exactly

    4096^2 *813^2 >=4097^2 *387*1707.

With h=m+1/2<4097 and rho=387/1707, use rho>1/5 and sqrt(rho)>2/5 in
the theorem's displayed expression. The resulting bound is below

    E_small=25*1707*(2*4097^5+3*4097)/6+5*4097/2
           =16420338088483447085805 <2^76.                  (3)

For any selected family whose chosen bad813-subsets have union at most1707,
pad that union to1707 using ONLY points already in the actual D and restrict
the SAME fixed sources to it. All witnesses still agree on their chosen
bad subsets. A putative K-polynomial lift of V on one of these subsets
would equal its unique F_p interpolant on388 of those actual base-field
points; both have degree below388. It would therefore be an F_p lift on
the entire bad subset, a contradiction.
The theorem therefore bounds the number of distinct full K-labels in that
family by (3). No query distribution, source, or evaluation domain of the
protocol is changed; padding is only a counting argument on actual points.

## 3. Apply these bounds to each regular matrix fiber

Use the exact setup and descended matrices T_j from
weighted-mca-small-stress-count.md, but assume dim W<=21. The chosen
polynomials f_i are cofactor columns for bad813-subsets of the entire
selected middle-support response family. Fix beta in F_p^5 and a regular
scalar fiber beta.alpha_i=t. Its columns lie in

    (T_beta-t Id)^(-1) W,

a space of dimension at most21. Span those ACTUAL columns; let its dimension
be r_f and let n_f be the union of their evaluation supports. Those supports
are exactly the selected bad813-subsets. Evaluating on the union gives a
code of minimum distance at least813, generated by this selected family.
Coordinates outside the union are identically zero on its span.

If n_f<=1707, use (3). If n_f>=1708, then n_f>2*813, so apply (1) with
ell=2. Since y/(y-1626) decreases for y>1626 and r_f<=21,

    m_f <= n_f/(n_f-1626) * A_813(r_f-2)
        <=1708/82 * A_813(19)
        =1708/82 *choose(831,18)
        =96521439648185566973505641095408249150
        =C_star.                                         (4)

The arithmetic checks C_star>2^76. Thus every regular fiber, including
the small-union branch, contains at most C_star full labels. Empty fibers
are immediate. Distinct labels have distinct cofactor columns by badness,
as in the preceding random-combination theorem.

## 4. Average regular fibers and retain the actual full-support weights

The independently reviewed random-combination argument now applies with
C_star replacing its coarse binomial C. For r=dim F<p, the determinant
that is identically zero in the random combination coefficients accounts
for at most r^5 labels. Each other label is regular with probability at
least1-r/p, whereas every beta has at most p*C_star regular labels.
Therefore

    m <= r^5+p^2*C_star/(p-r).                            (5)

This remains cardinality averaging of full labels, not a weighted
projection-max inference. The source-dependent regular fibers only need
the uniform bound (4), established separately for every beta and t.

Let p=2^64-2^32+1, N=2^23, r0=N-812, and retain one maximizing actual
full bad response for EVERY label whose maximum bad support lies in
813..56977. If some choice of one bad813-subset per such label has
dim W<=21, equation (5) gives

    M21=r0^5+floor(p^2*C_star/(p-r0))
       =1780506294402334431843840135889800907721994892128331979526.

Its diagnostic logarithm is190.18218894446989. With the original weight
wgt(g)=choose(g,20)/choose(N,20), use the unchanged full-support cap and
existing conservative tails:

    B <=p^5*wgt(812)+M21*wgt(56977)+2^44+2^36 <2^53.       (6)

The exact rational bound divided by2^53 is0.987570981428526 approximately.
No refined tail estimate is needed for (6).

The conclusion is conditional on the displayed stress-dimension premise.
Equivalently, any hypothetical unrestricted weighted-budget counterexample
must have dim W>=22 for EVERY bad813-subset choice of this ENTIRE selected
middle-support family. Nothing proves that such a counterexample exists,
that the high-stress family is impossible, or that the required small-stress
selection always exists.
